# Hepatobiliary surface appearance for laparoscopic synthesis

Research date: 2026-10-24. Scope: externally visible liver and gallbladder appearance, translated into authored synthetic rendering controls. This is a visual design reference, not a validated pathology simulator or a diagnostic classifier.

## Evidence-to-rendering matrix

The evidence column describes published observations. The rendering column is an implementation inference; its numeric choices below are authored assumptions, not clinical measurements.

| Condition | Directly supported visible features | Rendering inference | Confidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cirrhosis / substantial fibrosis | In a 101-patient laparoscopic-biopsy study, fibrosis correlated with surface irregularity, nodularity and a thicker edge. Only 12 of 20 histologically cirrhotic livers were grossly nodular; the other eight were irregular with heavy, thick edges. [Nudo et al., 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3093689/) | Use spatially uneven nodules and shallow connecting troughs, while retaining a continuous wet capsule. Support an irregular-but-not-obviously-nodular variant. Modestly alter edge shape independently of nodule contrast. | High for these observed features; the cohort was predominantly hepatitis C. Surface nodularity cannot identify exact histologic stage or cause. Do not make every severe liver a uniformly shrunken, knobbly sphere. |
| Steatotic enlargement / steatohepatitis | In 24 patients, common findings included mild enlargement, rounded edges, pale markings and fat around the round ligament; small surface depressions were common. Fibrosis was associated with a whiter and more uneven surface. [Tanaka et al., 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16420508/) | Separate fullness/edge rounding, diffuse warm pallor and fibrosis-related surface change. Restrict discrete external adipose to relevant surrounding structures; do not use fatty surface beads as a proxy for steatosis throughout the liver. | Moderate: small cohort, and steatohepatitis is not interchangeable with uncomplicated steatosis. Yellow intensity should not be a universal disease-severity meter. |
| Surface-visible HCC exemplar | A selected laparoscopic series of 22 yellow nodules reported focal yellow nodules and locally increased/new vascularity. Histology supplied the cellular findings used with the surface appearance. [Kameda and Shinji, 1992](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1327938/) | An asymmetric focal swelling can have a muted yellow-tan component and a localized irregular vascular halo. Preserve the surrounding liver material; offer a cirrhotic background independently. | Moderate for this particular visual phenotype, low as a general model of all HCC. A deep HCC need not leave a surface mark. Yellow nodules alone do not establish malignancy. |
| Surface metastasis exemplars | Capsular depressions over necrotic/fibrotic metastases were correlated with resection specimens; nearby adhesions did not necessarily represent invasion. [Weinbren et al., 1987](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3621815/) A separate surgical case described multiple whitish, well-circumscribed liver nodules. [In Vivo case report, 2021](https://iv.iiarjournals.org/content/35/5/2963) | Mix discrete pale subcapsular nodules of unequal sizes with a minority of shallow centrally retracted plaques. Maintain normal tissue between sites. Avoid identical concentric targets and automatic vessel growth over every lesion. | Moderate for umbilication; low for universal color/shape because the white-nodule example is a single case. Internal necrosis is not automatically visible through an intact capsule. |
| Simple capsular cyst | A surgical review describes thin-walled, fluid-filled sacs; its Figure 2 shows an actual large superficial cyst before and after fenestration. [Shimizu et al., 2022](https://www.nms.ac.jp/sh/jnms/2022/089010002.pdf) | A smoothly blended, tense dome with suppressed high-frequency normal detail and a coherent highlight is more appropriate than rough solid tumor material. A restrained grey/cool cast may be an artistic material choice, not a diagnostic color. | High for smooth fluid-filled morphology; no calibrated optical constants were provided. Do not infer the internal lining, cyst type, or benignity from external appearance alone. |
| Gallbladder inflammation | The original Parkland scale was derived from intraoperative images: limited neck adhesions can precede hyperemia, body adhesions/distension, extensive concealment and ultimately necrosis/perforation. [Madni et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amjsurg.2017.05.017) | Treat distension, patchy hyperemia, edema and adhesion coverage as related but distinct controls. More advanced examples should change the surrounding interface and what remains visible, not only the organ tint. | High for surgical appearance categories; the grading system is not a simple biological time sequence. Avoid presenting a render severity slider as a clinical Parkland score. |
| Distended / gangrenous gallbladder | A contemporary review's in-vivo Figure 2 explicitly shows swelling and dark purple-to-black serosa in gangrene. Other figures show varied distension, wall thickness and adjacent inflammatory change. [Patel et al., 2024](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/rg.240032) | Preserve the neck while expanding the body/fundus; use irregular dark patches for a separate advanced-complication exemplar. Necrotic areas should not have the same vivid vessel contrast as hyperemic viable areas. | High for the illustrated case, not prevalence. Distension and inflammation should remain separate parameters; mucosal and CT/US signs must not be copied literally onto the outside surface. |

## Current implementation findings

Reviewed `apps/studio/src/imports/variations.ts`, particularly `DISEASE_PROFILE_SPECS`, `bakeSeamlessImportedPbrMaps`, `buildImportedDiseaseField` and the organ-local application path.

1. The positive-displacement floor `minAnatomicalRadius = diagonal * 0.11` overrides the smaller authored cirrhosis (`0.02–0.04`) and metastatic (`0.032–0.072`) radius ranges. Different diseases consequently converge on broad swellings. The minimum should depend on mesh sampling and morphology, while sub-resolution detail belongs in normals.
2. All sites share a single dome-oriented field and a single target color. Cysts, nodules, retractions and inflammation need different combinations of displacement, color, roughness and local vascular visibility. The existing broad-envelope anti-crater safeguard remains useful for diffuse swelling, but should not forbid shallow metastatic umbilication.
3. `isSteatotic` sets `adiposeWeight` to `0.85`, generating external perivascular fat lobules across the liver material. The laparoscopic steatohepatitis study localizes conspicuous external fat to the round ligament; it does not support distributing visible fat beads over the whole capsule.
4. `isNodularOrFibrotic` also boosts liver artery/vein prominence and relief. The reviewed evidence does not support an obligatory prominent arterial/venous tree over every cirrhotic surface. Cap fine capsule-vessel contrast independently; make any focal HCC vascularity local.
5. The disease material cache currently keys the entire organ texture by profile and material seed. A profile-wide roughness/color change cannot align focal material pathology precisely with site geometry. Reuse the same rest-space site masks for lesion geometry and material channels.
6. Severity currently enters as `0.48 + 0.52 * severity`, and shape as `0.52 + 0.68 * shapeStrength` when positive. This gives nonzero baseline intensity even near zero. That may be intentional for selected disease exemplars; it should be documented rather than implying zero means unaffected tissue.

## Concrete authored parameter proposals

All numbers below are **art-direction starting points**, not measured tissue constants, distributions, staging thresholds or evidence of clinical fidelity. Let `D` be the organ-local bounding diagonal, `r` a site support radius, `s` normalized exemplar intensity, and `h` median local edge length. Keep geometry in organ rest space so the same fields can deform attached visible vessels. If `r < 2–3h`, represent that scale in normal detail or subdivide deliberately instead of forcing every site to `0.11D`.

| Family | Geometry starting point | Material and vessel starting point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cirrhotic irregularity | Distinct surface scales: geometry support radii `0.008–0.035D`, overlapping irregular normal detail below that; final nodule displacement `0.08–0.22r`. Mix two site-size bands and vary coverage instead of inflating every nodule together. Keep broad edge change separate. | Derive pale seam/valley modulation and roughness from the same nodularity field, but avoid a regular Voronoi grid. Keep baseline fine vessel contrast roughly `0.15–0.35` of the hollow-serosa value; do not increase all vessel relief with fibrosis. |
| Steatotic fullness | Modest anisotropic organ scaling around `1.03–1.16`, smooth convex fullness; reduce rather than add fine geometric nodules when isolated steatosis is selected. Combine fibrosis only as an explicit phenotype. | Diffuse warm pale admixture `0.10–0.30`; retain heterogeneous red-brown substrate and wet capsule highlights. Use external fat weight near baseline liver (`0.10–0.18`) rather than `0.85`; a separate ligament/omentum mask may carry fat. |
| Surface HCC exemplar | One dominant asymmetric swelling and optional smaller satellite; support `0.04–0.12D`, contour displacement `0.10–0.25r`. Keep normal displacement bounded and check attachment masks. | Local pale tan/yellow admixture `0.20–0.55`; vessel contrast near lesion rim `1.2–1.5×` local baseline, tapering to baseline outside `1.4r`. These multipliers are illustrative, not perfusion estimates. |
| Surface metastases exemplar | Unequal support radii `0.015–0.07D`, seeded separation and occasional groups. Mix low convex mounds with a selectable minority of shallow depressions: center retraction `0.03–0.08r`, broad feathered rim. | Ivory/tan lesion admixture `0.25–0.65`; same mask for normal smoothing, roughness and color. Avoid exact circular rings and uniformly white lesions. Subcapsular vessel visibility may be reduced over an opaque lesion instead of added everywhere. |
| Cyst | One smooth dome, support `0.04–0.12D`, final displacement `0.2–0.5r` if mesh sampling supports it. Blend slope into host surface with a smooth compact support function; avoid overlapping sites unless a multiple-cyst variant is selected. | Reduce high-frequency normal amplitude `70–90%` within the dome, roughness approximately `0.22–0.36` under the existing lighting. Any transmission must be bounded and exportable; do not turn the host organ transparent. |
| Gallbladder distension/inflammation | Expand the body/fundus transversely `1.08–1.30`, longitudinally `1.02–1.14`, tapering displacement to zero toward the neck/hilar attachment. Separate this shape control from inflammation. | Patchy red admixture `0.10–0.40`, vascular contrast `1.1–1.4×` in viable inflamed regions, and modest patchy roughness. Add pale adhesion/fibrin material only in spatially plausible attachment zones. For an explicit advanced-complication exemplar, irregular dark violet-brown patches can attenuate vessel visibility by `40–80%`. |

Surface masks should use position plus outward-facing normal agreement so a site on the anterior capsule does not color or displace the opposite side of a thin organ. Keep lesion material and deformation in the same coordinate frame. Keep color changes local, and preserve a consistent wet specular layer over underlying anatomical color variation.

Smooth glistening liver and lobulated fat create distinct adjacent materials. | Original PDF explicitly states Elsevier copyright, all rights reserved. Authored implication after image inspection: do not bake a generic dense vessel tree plus adipose lobules into every organ. Distinguish smooth capsule, semi-translucent stretched membrane, attached fat and discrete adhesions. In cysts, preserve fine surface vessels while smoothing the underlying tissue noise; the photograph does not support erasing all vascular detail. Use local material variation and isolated specular glints instead of a uniformly glossy plastic finish.

## Research quality and remaining uncertainty

Nine core publications informed this report: five original clinical investigations/case reports (Nudo, Tanaka, Kameda, Weinbren, the 2021 In Vivo case), two original surgical-image grading publications (Madni and Sugrue), and two academic reviews with directly attributable operative photographs (Shimizu and Patel). Primary abstracts were read for Tanaka, Kameda and Weinbren; relevant full-text sections/tables were read for Nudo, Madni, Sugrue and Shimizu, and the public abstract/operative figure captions for Patel. The In Vivo full-page fetch was blocked, so its limited color observation relies on the indexed article excerpt and is marked low confidence. Several PMC full-page fetches and the Tanaka repository PDF were also blocked; no unavailable content is claimed as inspected.

Original Madni full text read at [this PDF mirror](https://fcsanahuac.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/the-parkland-grading-scale-for-cholecystitis.pdf); canonical publication is [The Parkland grading scale for cholecystitis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amjsurg.2017.05.017). No source images were copied into project assets. The research tool returned screenshot placeholders, so the three image sources above were inspected through locally rendered PDFs instead.

The literature establishes families of visible features, not calibrated PBR values. No reviewed study supplies RGB reflectance, roughness, displacement spectra or population-level procedural distributions suitable for direct sampling. No clinical validation of the proposed render parameters is implied. Expert blinded comparison against appropriately licensed laparoscopic references would be required before describing these scenes as validated surgical training data.
