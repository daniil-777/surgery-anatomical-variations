# Abdominal disease phenotypes for laparoscopic Import 3D

Research date: 2026-10-24. Scope: visible outer-surface morphology of pancreas, bowel/mesentery, spleen and kidney. This is an evidence-to-rendering brief, not diagnostic or treatment guidance. The proposed shader and deformation mappings are authored approximations; none of the sources validates the generated scenes or supplies calibrated optical/mechanical parameters.

## Main finding

Realism depends on **where a change occurs, what anatomical layer is exposed, and which adjacent structures participate**. A pancreatic infiltrative mass, a smooth capsular cyst, a healed splenic infarct, and a Crohn-affected bowel segment should not share one spherical bump and one disease tint. The available evidence most strongly supports separate phenotype operators with shared geometry/material masks and preserved organ boundaries.

## Existing implementation reviewed

Read `apps/studio/src/imports/variations.ts`, `py/viscera_core/disease_profiles.json`, relevant entries in `disease_profiles_v2.json`, and the disease field/eligible-vertex logic in `atlas_disease.py`.

- `pancreas-pancreatitis` means **chronic calculous pancreatitis**, pattern `fibrosis`, with negative global scale; `pancreas-diffuse-swelling` is a separate inflammatory enlargement phenotype. These must remain distinct.
- `spleen-infarct` means **healed** infarction. Its source targets wedge-shaped territory, scar distribution and capsule involvement.
- The original Crohn source is a low-resolution jejunal specimen. The profile explicitly says external creeping fat and fistulas are not represented; its mucosal target must not be interpreted as an outer-serosal target.
- `kidney-cancer` cites a sectioned specimen; the existing profile does not establish histologic subtype. Its morphology targets already distinguish endophytic/exophytic extent and collecting-system relationships.
- The source metadata correctly calls these authored outer-surface prototypes, not paired 3D disease supervision. Preserve that provenance.
- `buildImportedDiseaseField()` currently gives every disease the same normal-directed organic support operator. Positive radius is clamped to at least 0.11 of the organ diagonal and negative radius to 0.16; this can erase the intended small cyst/diverticular scale. It chooses Euclidean rather than anatomical/geodesic support, which can affect neighboring bowel loops that happen to be close in space.
- Several texture branches test pattern `fibrotic`, whereas the chronic pancreatic profile is `fibrosis`. Audit this vocabulary before claiming disease-specific fibrotic appearance.
- Current texture synthesis is broadly organ-aware but its pathology response is mostly global preset/colour modulation. Disease-specific surface detail must use the same local support as geometry, not simply switch the entire organ's repeating texture.

## Deep-read notes and limits

1. **Liu et al. 2025 issue / 2024 online, Gastroenterology:** examined introduction, human resection procurement/definitions, Figure 1 and discussion/limitations. This is human surgical tissue plus mechanistic cell/mouse work, not a calibrated laparoscopic image dataset. The subserosal fat–muscle relationship is the useful spatial evidence; cellular mechanism is not a shader parameter.
2. **APA 2014 pathology consensus, Topic 2:** examined definitions, gross features and Figures 1–7 with regional versus diffuse involvement. Useful evidence is retained/scarred lobular organization; its duct and histologic images are not external texture maps.
3. **ECCO/ESP 2013, §4.1:** read the gross feature discussion against the following microscopic section. The explicit distinction between mucosal cobblestoning and serosal findings is critical for this project.
4. **Macheras et al. 2005:** read all three cases and the beginning of the discussion, especially the laparoscopic cyst roof and histologic wall description. A single external cyst morphology cannot establish epithelial subtype.

Sources were sought through web search because Exa/Firecrawl were not configured. Primary surgical papers, consensus statements and university specimen collections were prioritized; the splenic healing review and educational renal material provide supporting morphology. Some web-tool PMC pages returned browser challenges; the publicly served HTML, Europe PMC XML and NCBI image CDN were subsequently accessible through direct downloads. Images were inspected from a temporary research folder; none was copied into application assets.

## Implementation priorities inferred from the evidence

1. Add a small set of distinguishable operators: smooth subcapsular dome, infiltrative broad mass, shallow territorial scar, segmental bowel remodeling, and fat wrapping connected to a mesenteric boundary. Keep sample variation inside each operator rather than letting all diseases become the same bump field.
2. Share one deterministic rest-space disease mask between deformation, colour, normal detail, roughness and vessel attenuation/prominence. For bowel, measure interval distance along the bowel centerline and angular distance from its mesenteric side; Euclidean spheres are insufficient around folded loops.
3. Maintain separate normal anatomy and local pathology scales: organ contour, lobular/fat relief, vessel arcades, then fine tissue detail. Do not add visible histologic cell patterns or an equally prominent branching vessel tree to every organ.
4. Vary geometry and appearance coherently: a taut cyst roof should lose fine lobular relief; chronic fibrosis should suppress lobulation and contract selected regions; fat wrapping should add real contour volume, not yellow paint; a scar should retract rather than inflate.
5. Validate paired views of the **same seed**: normal, mild and stronger authored phenotype, under neutral and surgical lighting. Require preserved organ identity, plausible attachments, shared texture/shape localization, and visible differences between the five operators. PBR numbers, severity-to-size functions and exact colour palettes still require artist/surgeon review; these sources do not determine them quantitatively.

Remaining gap: no reviewed source establishes a disease-specific intact external appearance for the generic `spleen-exophytic-mass` or `pancreas-multicystic-surface` profiles. Keep those labels morphological and avoid assigning a diagnosis solely from their procedural appearance.
