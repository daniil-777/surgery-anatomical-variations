# Disease-v2: bounded external phenotype design

This release adds 60 supported external phenotype designs across 12 organ groups, while preserving the 25 previously supported IDs and retaining 11 internal/topology-changing reference-only entries. `renderer_version: "disease-v2"` is required. V1 sources, datasets and model-bound algorithms are unchanged.

Supported means a renderer can construct an authored outer-surface proxy. It does not mean disease diagnosis, clinical stage, tissue mechanics, pathological internal anatomy or validation against patient surfaces. The `mild`, `moderate` and `severe` bands mean requested design strength only. Profile records explicitly state represented and missing features.

## Geometry and source limits

Each source mesh retains its vertex count, components and triangle connectivity. Disjoint spherical compact C2 displacement fields compose 5–7 times, then a positive axis-scale envelope and the existing seven-control atlas map apply. The maximum kernel derivative is `96/(25*sqrt(5))/radius`. Each step has a derivative bound below one; a positive continuous Jacobian lower bound is recorded.

This continuous-map guarantee alone does not protect coarse or sliver source triangles. Every accepted recipe therefore checks every source face against its exact inverse-transpose transported normal, evaluated at the original triangle centroid. Finite triangles must retain area and positive orientation in that transported frame in float64 millimetres, Studio float32 millimetres, and the glTF float32-metre roundtrip. The complete local displacement and global envelope are bounded together when the discrete mesh cannot represent the requested field. Capacity is measured independently of requested severity. Requested strength multiplies that capacity, so mild and severe requests use a common geometric gain even when source tessellation restricts the maximum. Float32 quantization can invalidate an intermediate strength; a shared deterministic backoff then halves effective strength until the actual output passes. This rare fallback is recorded and may break strict monotonicity. Requested and effective strength, local/global fractions, minimum alignment and area ratios are recorded. Source self-intersections and organ-level anatomical validity are not certified.

For dataset construction and v2 serving, each template/family shape draw first receives a deterministic source-safe projection of its seven shape controls. Baseline and all disease descendants use and store those same applied controls. Disease layouts that cannot pass the guard are deterministically resampled, preserving the paired material seed and actual renderer seed in the record. Serving and export run the same shape projection and full-face guard, return the actual applied controls in the bound recipe, and fail explicitly if no certified output exists. Final dataset rejections are retained separately after 16 unsuccessful layout attempts, and every condition must retain at least 95% of its requested rows.

The v2 release uses a separately versioned `templates-v3` directory. Two vertices are conditioned in their original triangle planes: male-heart vertex 58832 moves 0.0000387551 mm (38.76 nm); male-bladder vertex 2634 moves 0.000119314 mm (119.3 nm), stabilizing two shared sliver faces. This prevents a submicron triangle from reversing under float32-mm conversion. The exact deltas, parent hashes and affected faces are recorded in `precision-repair.json`, and the archive retains the parent vertices. This is numerical precision conditioning, not recovered anatomy. All previous templates and v1 sources remain byte-identical; the portable archive schema stays 1.1.0 with explicit derived-release metadata.

The support radius is at least three times the source 90th-percentile edge length. This limits feature width relative to typical source edges; it does not guarantee uniform tessellation quality. It cannot manufacture detail absent from the original source: BodyParts3D stomach/gallbladder references remain coarse; the brain contains component seams and sliver triangles. Some requested severe brain lesions therefore require substantial geometric attenuation; a severe requested band is not a guarantee of severe realized geometry.

Source capsule meshes can contain inward-facing inner layers. New lesion anchor sites use outward-facing source support; kidney capsule sites are preferred over the hilum. A mild outward radial component stabilizes the authored displacement direction. Source normals are not flipped or edited. Single-cyst and circumscribed-mass designs are capped to one site; multifocal and nodular designs retain distinct counts. Diffuse enlargement/atrophy depends primarily on positive global envelope scaling rather than repeated tumor-like bulges.

Shading normals are source smooth normals transported by the exact inverse-transpose continuous Jacobian. The same arbitrary-point evaluator supplies full mesh and dataset queries. Finite-difference tests check both disease and combined atlas Jacobians. Source limitations and numerical QA do not establish physical realism.

## Actual evidence use

Eleven profile color targets use permitted gross-photo tissue ROI observations. Each profile stores its original authored target, exact observed median, ROI/source SHA256, license, compartment and blend weight. The authored target is blended by 50% with the external polycystic-kidney ROI median, or by 25% with cut/mucosal ROI medians for other profiles. This is an explicit design transfer, not tissue albedo calibration or paired photo-to-mesh supervision. The observed RGB values include illumination, preservation and camera effects.

The evidence review also preserves internal clinical masks, physical-size descriptors and their rights lanes. Internal lesion volumes are not equated to an external displacement or clinical severity. The new CT masks and measured lesion volumes were not used to sample geometry or produce training targets; they remain separate review evidence.

## Paired dataset and model contract

The final release contains 13,824 baseline and 69,120 disease anchor records, with no rejected rows: 576 draws for each of 24 baseline and 120 disease/template conditions. Every record stores 512 distinct source vertex queries, 22 normalized controls, normalized rest/posed XYZ, linear RGB, roughness, transported normals, material-region IDs, lesion masks and exact source vertex IDs. The disease row also contains baseline observations at identical queries with identical shape/material controls and material seed.

Two explicit uint32 seeds control material and disease phase. Their eight little-endian bytes form lossless normalized context features. Query sites vary by row and include component/extents, area-weighted coverage and lesion support. They are not a fixed ordered anatomical vector. The manifest field `lesion_visible_anchor_count` counts queries whose procedural mask exceeds 0.01; it is not a guarantee of optical visibility or clinical detectability.

All descendants of a template/family draw, including baseline and every disease profile, inherit one split. Severity is stratified within those families. Templates are shared across splits: this tests interpolation within the authored renderer, not unseen donors, anatomy scans or disease populations. The independent patient count is zero.

Generation uses disk-backed arrays and streamed standard `train.npz`, `val.npz`, `audit.npz` archives. No full-resolution mesh is stored for each training row. Every manifest row records full-source face QA, supplemented by a separate 720-case boundary/visual audit. Neither check is a collision, mechanical, clinical or population validation.

Every release binds exact renderer, profile catalog, template, template metadata, builder and artifact hashes. The renderer is frozen before generation. Material colors remain arbitrary-point rest-space fields; shader/export color masks preserve the previous spherical-field contract.
