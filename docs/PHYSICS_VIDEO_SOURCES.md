# Surgical video and mechanics sources

Reviewed 7 October 2026. This is a targeted survey of 16 source families, not an exhaustive list. Dataset reuse terms are recorded separately from code and paper licenses. Public viewing does not establish training rights.

The acquired pilot is one **CC BY 4.0 ex-vivo chicken-skin dVRK recording**, with stereo images, measured force/torque and robot Cartesian pose. It can support experiments on apparent tool–tissue response; it cannot establish human abdominal-organ material properties.

## Pilot files and exact acquisition

`physics-releases/source-pilot/raw/M1_L_V1_1.bag` is the original 281,605,220-byte download from the [Zenodo file endpoint](https://zenodo.org/api/records/21041361/files/M1_L_V1_1.bag/content). Its publisher MD5 is `61d598d3c62631d16410a49194466d44`; verified SHA-256 is `cf3d11db744fefcd443718c7b4400ab1b9786b67531c81208e0d05259652eb16`. No login, form, payment or second recording was needed.

The sequence folder contains 1,747 original left and 1,746 original right JPEG payloads (960 × 540), raw numeric streams, synchronized arrays, a contact sheet, an MP4 preview and provenance. All camera/force header stamps are zero, so synchronization uses bag **record timestamps**, with residuals preserved. The nearest pose and force record offsets reach 4.30 ms and 3.47 ms; right-image offsets reach 33.9 ms. These numbers do not measure hardware latency. The corrected MP4 preserves all 1,747 left images once, with microsecond PTS (maximum encoding residual 0.499 microseconds) and a +0.023696026-second bag-time offset. Three source record intervals are only 14–44 microseconds, indicating recorder batching rather than camera exposure cadence. The MP4 is for review; arrays/JPEG timestamps remain authoritative.

`signals_raw.npz` retains recording/header nanoseconds, relative times, 55,893 Cartesian poses and 59,706 measured force/torque samples. `synchronized.npz` follows left-image timestamps and includes `t_s`, `position_m`, `orientation_xyzw`, `force_n`, `torque_nm`, source indices, timing residuals and `valid_timing`. It contains no fitted stiffness labels. ROS SI units and the author extraction convention are retained exactly; force calibration was not independently repeated.

The PSM Cartesian frame is `PSM1`. The force-sensor and camera frame IDs are empty. No camera intrinsics, stereo extrinsics or force-to-robot transform are in the acquired bag. Never dot the force vector into a robot/world displacement direction without independently verified registration. No constitutive parameter fit is promoted; `calibration-readiness.json` explicitly fails the required calibration, contact, model-admissibility and rest-geometry gates. The 15 automatically suggested loading windows use force magnitude only and are **not contact ground truth**; the final truncated window must be excluded. Video shows multiple pulls with changing directions.

The [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18172) reports plastic skin deformation and unreliable stiffness estimates for some demonstrations. Low force can coexist with a gripped, stretched surface. For this pilot, separate apparent loading response from true stress relaxation, slip, grip release, camera motion and plasticity. The default source partition is the authors’ seen-material/seen-view training group, specimen `M1`; any held-out cycles from the same bag are only temporal validation. An unseen-specimen test would require keeping the entire M2/M4 specimen groups unused for fitting.

## What to add next

1. **D4D** for abdominal surface motion: it includes porcine liver, bowel and abdominal-wall manipulations with independent structured-light reference geometry. The small public `info.tar.gz` metadata was inspected; full specimen archives were not downloaded. Keep specimen/session/clip grouping and preserve the source quality flags.

2. **MULTIS** for better-conditioned inverse mechanics: measured force, registered tool motion, surface strain and linked imaging. It uses human cadaver limb tissue rather than abdominal organs; exact tool geometry, freeze/thaw history, boundary conditions and timing uncertainty must be retained. Dataset license was verified at the archive itself, independently from the paper.

3. **STIR/SurgT** for tracker validation and **Cholec80/CholecT50** for clinical appearance/action context. These do not supply organ force/modulus labels. Cholec sources are noncommercial; their shared videos must be deduplicated.

## Reproduce and inspect

```bash
uv pip install --python /path/to/python numpy rosbags==0.11.5
python scripts/extract_physics_pilot.py \
  physics-releases/source-pilot/raw/M1_L_V1_1.bag \
  /tmp/new-physics-sequence
```

The extractor rejects an existing output directory, retains original compressed image bytes, and does not smooth, interpolate or fit signals. It extracts PSM1 for this selected sequence; another arm requires an explicit topic configuration change. The complete [machine-readable catalog](../references/physics/source_catalog.json) records modalities, data/code rights, split units, source URLs and evidence hashes. Research evidence copies are for local provenance review; they do not grant broader redistribution rights to papers or websites. The pilot attribution/manifest identifies the CC BY media that may be redistributed with attribution.
