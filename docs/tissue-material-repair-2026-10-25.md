# Tissue material repair

The recommended model was a three-update engineering fixture. Applying it replaced the authored organ texture with nearly neutral RGB. Gallery thumbnails referenced files omitted from the public asset closure. The browser fallback generated low-resolution periodic ridges and incorrectly described itself as learned diffusion inference.

The default now uses a compact, nonparametric exemplar model fitted to four verified 512×512 learned tiles per organ: liver, spleen, stomach, pancreas, small bowel and large bowel. Browser workers vary periodic coordinates and blend decorrelated, Gaussianized appearance samples with variance normalization, then invert the fitted empirical distributions. This keeps organ-specific colors and fine source detail. It does not train a new neural network. Public UI name: **Generative Model 13**; internal method: **PCA histogram-preserving exemplar synthesis**. Existing model names and internal provenance remain unchanged.

`fit_tissue_exemplars.py` reproduces the six checksum-bound model exports from the existing clean learned tiles. Inputs retain their source hashes, training provenance and research-use restrictions. Relief and roughness in browser-generated bundles are bounded authored estimates derived from the same appearance. Broad source-image shadows do not become large geometric deformations. No semantic vessel, fat or fascia segmentation is inferred. The separate fat pipeline is untouched.

The synthesis design follows the distribution-preserving blending principle described by [Heitz and Neyret](https://eheitzresearch.wordpress.com/722-2/) and [their HPG 2018 paper](https://www.highperformancegraphics.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/Papers-Session3/HPG2018_ByExampleNoise.pdf). This implementation uses two dominant periodic exemplars and bounded warping; it is not a reproduction of the paper's complete stochastic tiling method. [Efros and Freeman's image quilting work](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~efros/research/quilting.html) informed the choice to preserve example texture structures rather than smooth them into a small undertrained decoder.

Saved early fixtures are no longer automatically applied. Explicit experimental imports remain available. Default selection does not silently substitute another organ. Gallery images use verified base-color files that are part of the asset closure. Browser generation is directly available and supports cancellation, complete uint64 seeds and integrity-checked PBR imports.

Validation artifacts record rendered RGB, normal and roughness views, multiple organs, seed replay, thumbnails, responsive layouts, native-library integrity, live 4K timing and offline walkthrough encoding. These are engineering checks, not a surgeon assessment, recovered human optics or clinical qualification. Learned source appearance is 512×512; a 4K video does not add texture detail.

Final acceptance on 2026-10-25 passed TypeScript, 208 unit checks, 162 unique Python contracts, 38 browser checks and six whole-app smoke checks. The native snapshot audit passed all 21 materials. Final frozen-source review completed 73 rendered cases and seven 3840×2160 videos without browser errors. Adjacent material seeds differed; replaying seed 42 was pixel-identical and preserved geometry. Earlier failures and corrected reruns remain in the local evidence directory.

Observed interactive 4K RAF intervals were 16.7 ms median and 16.8 ms p95 on this Mac. Live 4K recordings encoded about 24.7–30.0 frames per second depending on the view; the separate deterministic walkthrough was encoded offline at 24 fps. These are different measurements, not a sustained-performance guarantee. Loading/applying the new appearance took about 1.0 s and creating a complete 512×512 browser material about 1.6 s in the focused local check.

Visual inspection covered all six fitted organ appearances, vessel detail, grazing light, dark regions, native organ exteriors, mucosal walkthroughs and successive video frames. The pale-liver regression is resolved and gallery previews decode correctly. Existing connective-tissue geometry and source-image baked illumination remain approximations; they have not been clinically validated. Public previews and fitted appearance are 512×512, while authored native materials remain available locally at 4096×4096.
