# Expanded disease generation: delivery contract

This release adds a conditional spline-flow model for coupled shape, colour/material and disease-design variation. The previous joint VAE, original Colab weights and original rendering behavior remain available. It does not train a new text model or measured tissue physics.

## What the datasets contain

The completed build contains **82,944 synthetic examples**: 13,824 baseline examples and 69,120 disease examples. There are 24 attributed HRA/BodyParts3D templates, 120 template/disease conditions, and 512 distinct source-vertex queries per example. The catalog has **60 supported external phenotype designs across 12 organ groups**, five per group. Those are not 60 clinically validated disease-stage models.

The final train/validation/audit split contains 57,888 / 12,528 / 12,528 examples. All 82,944 accepted rows passed full-source mesh checks, with zero rejected rows. This is 11.68 times the previous Colab model's 7,104 examples and 46.70 times its surface-query observations. Exact release and archive identities are recorded in `release-index.json`.

Each row pairs a deterministic source-surface deformation with its rendered colour and roughness. Exact recipes, source vertex IDs, phase seeds, template licenses and hashes are retained. Parent families stay in one split across baseline and disease profiles. Templates are shared across splits: this measures generalization within the authored generator, not performance on unseen patient anatomy. Independent measured patients in these training rows: **zero**.

The default exported flow learns the joint distribution of the 22 bounded synthetic recipe controls, pooled by healthy versus disease type. Its template/profile/organ residual context gain is zero. Template selection and the phenotype-specific renderer still determine organ shape and disease appearance. The paired surface/RGB/roughness observations train a separate diagnostic field surrogate; its flow regularizer weight is zero by default, so those observations do not backpropagate into the exported flow. The surrogate is not deployed in Studio. This release does not claim a learned mesh/colour decoder or organ-specific patient distributions.

## Which new evidence is actually used

Eleven profile palettes now use observed tissue ROI values in the actual renderer and hence in paired colour observations. The exact formula is `(1-w) × previous authored sRGB + w × observed RGB / 255`, with `w=0.50` for the external polycystic-kidney reference and `w=0.25` for declared compartment/proxy transfers. Each profile retains source hash, license, ROI ID, original target and blend weight. Stored photographic RGB is not calibrated albedo.

The separate evidence archive includes 40 visual assets, 14 reviewed ROI patches, 20 new CT label cases and three prior pancreatic cases. **Those CT masks are not consumed by this release's training sampler, geometry targets or measured deformation bounds.** They remain a separate reference/rights lane. This actual-use statement supersedes intended `training_shape_guidance` roles in the frozen acquisition manifest. `artifacts/joint-v2/evidence_usage_receipt.json` records the implemented distinction without changing source evidence hashes. Paired measured photo/mesh cases, clinical severity labels and force/displacement supervision remain zero.

## Geometry and severity limits

Mild, moderate and severe are explicit **synthetic design-strength bands**, not medical staging. They control the magnitude of an authored surface phenotype. Internal tumors, ducts, mucosal architecture, invasion and histological detail are not generated by an external-envelope edit.

The revised renderer uses bounded continuous maps, a positive continuous-Jacobian lower bound, unchanged source connectivity and checks against transported source-face normals. It tests authored float64 positions, Studio float32 millimetres and GLB float32 metres. Source-limited capacity scales the requested strength; any additional precision reduction is recorded. The final cohort and 720-case boundary audit receipts identify the exact checked source and template hashes. The contact review includes 240 rendered mild/severe front/back panels; all twelve front sheets were visually inspected. Every dataset row received numerical checks, not individual manual visual review. Earlier RC1 audit results do not certify this revised release.

Source queries and bound recipes must reproduce the complete scene, and the final model export audits that replay for every sampled condition. These checks do not certify self-intersection freedom, anatomical validity, physical material behavior or surgical realism. Source defects and limited source detail remain relevant. The separately attributed v2 template derivatives retain original connectivity and record each submicron precision adjustment; these are numerical repairs, not recovered anatomical detail.

## Run All and return the weights

1. Put the completed baseline, disease and runtime ZIPs beside the notebook in **VISCERA Expanded Disease Training RC2 2026-10-07** in Google Drive.
2. Open **VISCERA_expanded_disease_run_all.ipynb** in Colab, choose an available GPU (A100 preferred), and choose **Run all**. Google may request Drive authorization.
3. The notebook verifies each pinned archive, creates an isolated environment, trains with the configured flow and renderer-surrogate stages, evaluates held-out distributions and audits every full-mesh condition/severity band. Interrupted stages have checksummed resume identity.
4. A successful final cell verifies and downloads **viscera-joint-v2-weights.zip**. The output and reports remain in the Drive run directory. A failed check must not be treated as a completed export.
5. In Studio, open **Trained models → Validate & activate**, import that ZIP, choose an organ/disease and **Disease design range**, then generate a joint variation. The resulting surface remains editable and can be exported as textured GLB.

Old bundles remain under **Preserved joint models**. Activating a retained bundle revalidates its model, source and dataset contracts. A corrupt active bundle does not hide other retained versions. Versioned recipes carry `renderer_version: disease-v2`; old recipes without the marker continue using the original renderer. Importing v2 does not erase old weights or retrain the separate text adapter. Explicit v2 disease text uses the guarded deterministic grammar; the existing learned language adapter remains limited to its separately validated shape-edit scope.

For a local API deployment, set `VISCERA_ATLAS_TEMPLATES_V2` to the installed v2 template directory whose NPZ and metadata hashes match the final runtime archive. Keep `VISCERA_ATLAS_TEMPLATES` pointing at the original templates for retained models. The v2 catalog, compile, generation, export and model importer use the v2 setting; a missing or mismatched v2 reference fails closed. Colab uses its checksummed `runtime/templates` directory automatically. The final v2 template lineage includes a separately recorded source-vertex repair; original v1 source files are preserved.
