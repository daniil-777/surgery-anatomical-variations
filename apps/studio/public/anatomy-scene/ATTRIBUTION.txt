Modifications: Shared atlas coordinate rotation/translation, component selection, schematic display colors, coincident-vertex normal welding. Heart/lungs are explicitly approximate cross-atlas HRA fits. Liver is a reconstructed visual exterior, generated using a 1 mm voxel grid, closing/fill, Gaussian-smoothed signed distance field, marching cubes and Taubin smoothing. This display reconstruction has no anatomical-fidelity or physiological guarantee. Mesentery and additional native mesenteric, renal and splenic vascular branches retain source registration. A separate bowel ambient-visibility attribute adds bounded self-organ diffuse shading without changing coordinates or topology; it is an authored display-lighting approximation. Original geometry, comparison evidence and regeneration scripts retained; full details in manifest.json and viscera/references/whole-scene/README.md.

Reference images: OpenStax College, Anatomy & Physiology (2013), CC BY 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/; Gray plate 1086, Henry Vandyke Carter (1918), public domain. Original image bytes unchanged.
