MODIFICATIONS
Prepared a fixed lobe section, split source skin at that plane, and embedded it
in a remeshed enclosing tetrahedral support cage with paired cohesive nodes.
The source skin coordinates, colors and connectivity were retained during
remeshing. The source section cap was re-embedded into the new tetrahedra.
Added a separate compact contact tessellation within the same tetrahedra;
rendered cap, continuous surface validator and mechanical degrees of freedom
are unchanged by contact compaction.

The selected reference is liver-reference.json, SHA-256
5289378fc753cc6f046d587f7933eefbdcd4b642e1d4fb0c3c38592c83d2f0d0.
The earlier coarse reference remains in original-coarse-reference.json.
Source attribution, diagnostics and compaction provenance are also in JSON.

OFFLINE TOOL
Gmsh 4.15.2, https://gmsh.info/, was used as a separate offline meshing tool.
No Gmsh binaries or source are included in this asset directory or web runtime.

SCOPE
This is an experimental prepared-interface simulation with a synthetic cohesive
calibration model. Numerical qualification is bounded to the tested geometry
and paths; it is not validation of patient-specific or clinical cutting behavior.
